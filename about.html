About us

On your side when the worst happens.

We're an independent team of loss assessors helping homeowners and businesses across the UK recover from fire and flood, and get the insurance settlement they deserve.

Who we are

Independent experts who work for you

When your home or business is damaged by fire or flood, your insurer appoints a loss adjuster to assess the claim on its behalf. We represent the other side: you, the policyholder.

Our assessors understand buildings, contents, business interruption and the fine print of insurance policies. We prepare a detailed claim, deal with the insurer and its adjuster, and negotiate so nothing you're entitled to is overlooked.

Just as importantly, we're a steady point of contact through a stressful time, arranging emergency help, keeping you updated and taking the paperwork off your hands.

Adjuster or assessor?

Works for the insurer

Loss adjuster

Appointed and paid by the insurance company to assess the claim for them.

Works for you

Loss assessor (that's us)

Appointed by you to prepare, manage and negotiate your claim in your interest.

How it works

A clear process from start to finish

We keep things simple so you always know what's happening with your claim.

  1. Free assessment

    Get in touch and we'll visit to inspect the damage and review your policy, at no cost.

  2. Emergency help

    We arrange urgent work such as drying, boarding up or temporary accommodation.

  3. Claim preparation

    We document every loss in detail and submit a complete, well-evidenced claim.

  4. Settlement

    We negotiate with your insurer and push for a full, fair settlement on your behalf.
