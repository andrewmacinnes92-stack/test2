Our story

A family tradition in loss assessment

It's over 100 years since the Castleton family first entered the complex and specialised world of loss assessment. It was at the family's instigation that the Institute of Public Loss Assessors was established, and Nic Castleton's grandfather was chosen as its president.

Nic founded Independent Claims Consultants three decades ago. Since then, the firm has helped individuals and businesses recover from a wide range of disasters, with every client assigned a dedicated loss assessor.

When you make a claim, your insurer appoints a loss adjuster to work in its interests. We work only in yours, using our expertise to get you the best possible settlement.

How we help

We manage every aspect of your claim

The earlier you appoint us, the more we can do. The best time is at the start of your claim, when your insurer appoints its loss adjuster.

  1. Secure your property

    We organise emergency works and arrange alternative accommodation where it's needed.

  2. Assess the full damage

    We identify the full extent of the damage to both your buildings and contents.

  3. Prepare and negotiate

    We prepare, present and negotiate your entire claim with your insurer and its loss adjuster.

  4. Oversee the repairs

    We help appoint specialist surveyors and contractors, and supervise their work through to completion.
