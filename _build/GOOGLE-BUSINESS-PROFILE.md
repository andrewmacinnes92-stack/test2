# Google Business Profile: Southern office (Cobham)

Everything to copy into the profile. Set it up at https://business.google.com while signed in to the Google account the business will own.

The profile is what puts you in Google Maps and in the map results for searches such as "loss assessor near me" or "loss assessor Surrey". Reviews are the biggest factor in how high you appear.

## Before you start

- **Use the exact business name.** It's "Independent Claims Consultants". Don't add keywords such as "Loss Assessors Surrey", because Google can suspend profiles for that.
- **A local phone number helps.** A Cobham (01932) number for this profile matches the address and builds trust. The 0161 number works, but a local number is better. You can switch later.
- **The head office needs its own profile.** Hale should have a separate profile with its own address. Check whether one already exists first, so you don't create a duplicate.

## Getting reviews

1. Once the profile is verified, open it and click **Ask for reviews**. Copy the short link.
2. Send the link to recent clients when their claim settles. That's when they're happiest.
3. Reply to every review, briefly and personally. Never offer anything in return for a review, because that breaks Google's rules.

Message you can send by text or email:

> Hi [name], it was a pleasure helping with your claim. If you were happy with our service, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps other people find independent help when they need it: [review link]. Thank you, [your name], Independent Claims Consultants

## Verification

Google will ask you to prove you're at the address. This is usually done by a short video of the office and signage, a postcard or a phone call. Only you can do this step, so plan to be at the office when you set it up.
